Help

The model reads 312 binary attribute flags per bird photo (shape, color, habitat cues) and predicts one of 200 species. Training uses 8,000 attribute rows; your picks come from the remaining holdout set.

Choose a sample chip or enter an image ID, then Classify. Compare the top predicted species with the known label shown after scoring.

First launch trains a random forest and caches it under web/data/cache. If metadata files are missing, run python scripts/fetch_cub_metadata.py from the demo root.
